## 1. Data Sources — Real Deployment

| Source | Data Type | Access Pathway | Variables Used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Echo reporting database (e.g. structured measurement export, as in MIMIC-IV-ECHO `structured_measurement`) | serial TTE measurements with exam date and indication | institutional warehouse; nightly extract; DICOM SR where available | mean/peak gradient, peak velocity, EOA, DVI, LVEF, stroke-volume index, intraprosthetic and paravalvular regurgitation grade |
| STS Adult Cardiac Surgery Database | SAVR procedure and discharge data | site participant files; STS research access | valve model, UDI, size, comorbidities, BMI, pre/post LVEF, discharge anticoagulant |
| STS/ACC TVT Registry | TAVR procedure, 30-day and 1-y echo | TVT research proposal | device model and size, discharge and 30-day gradient/EOA, PVL, valve thrombosis, reintervention |
| EHR (structured + notes) | problem list, labs, medications, operative and echo notes | FHIR / warehouse; transformer NLP for free text (`E1`) | AF, anticoagulant type and dates, creatinine/dialysis, smoking, symptoms, valve model when uncoded, reintervention indication |
| CMS claims and National Death Index | out-of-system reintervention and death | CMS DUA; NDI application | ViV/redo procedure codes and dates, date of death |

Integration dependency: a patient- and device-level linkage key across the echo database, the STS/TVT record and the EHR.

## 3. Availability Assumptions

- Follow-up echo attendance of about 91%, 85%, 78% and 65% at years 1–4 (PERIGON, `A3`); real-world completeness beyond 5 y is lower. **High risk** for community-followed patients; mitigated by claims linkage for hard endpoints.
- Valve model and size coded in STS/TVT or recoverable from operative notes. **High risk** in EHR-only sites: in the provided notes the model was named in 97 of 117 patients, but de-identification sometimes removed the brand.
- A reference echo within 3 months for about 70% of valves. **High risk**: only about 5 of 117 provided notes had a numeric post-implant gradient; the model falls back to model × size norms with a flag.
- EOA or DVI reported in ≥90% of echoes (needed for the full VARC-3 definition).
- Exact exam and procedure dates available under a limited data set (year-only dates, as in the provided extracts, make interval analysis impossible).
- Death ascertainment complete through linkage.
- Measured on the provided notes (117 patients): valve model named in 92%, size in 97%, BMI in 47%, a prosthetic echo with a mean gradient in 36%, prosthetic gradients in two or more years in 2%, numeric age in 0% (redacted). Serial structured echo data therefore has to come from the echo database, not from notes.

## 4. Preprocessing and Data Quality

### Data Cleaning
Duplicates removed on patient, exam date and modality, keeping the finalised report. Units harmonised (velocity m/s vs cm/s; EOA cm²; gradients mmHg). Range checks: mean gradient 1–100 mmHg (winsorised at 100), EOA 0.4–4 cm², DVI 0.1–1.0, LVEF 10–85%; out-of-range values set to missing and logged. TTE preferred over TEE for serial comparison. Free-text reports and operative notes parsed with a transformer extraction model (`E1`) and hand-validated on a 200-report sample.

### Missing Data Strategy
Expected missingness: follow-up echoes 9–35% by year 1–4, EOA 8% and DVI 5% of echoes, reference echo ~30%. Missing channel values are kept as missing, with indicator features, for the boosted model; the Cox model uses median imputation plus indicators. Missed visits are represented by time since last echo and echo count rather than imputed. Valves without any follow-up echo are excluded (§2 of the protocol).

### Temporal Alignment
Time zero is the implant date. The reference echo is the first TTE between 7 days and 3 months after implant (the discharge/30-day echo is accepted). Every later echo is a landmark; features at a landmark use only echoes on or before it. Prediction horizons: 0.5–5 y from each landmark in 6-month periods and 5/8/10 y from implant. Follow-up ends at death, reintervention, loss to follow-up or the data cut-off.

### Label / Ground Truth Construction
For each follow-up echo, the VARC-3 stage is computed against the reference echo. A label is confirmed when the next evaluable echo meets the same or a higher stage; the event time is the first echo of the pair for incidence and the confirming echo for dynamic prediction. Reintervention for SVD before confirmation is an event; endocarditis voids later labels. Prevalence in the prototype: confirmed stage ≥2 in 1,641 of 10,000 valves (cumulative incidence 6.2%, 11.3% and 16.8% at 5, 8 and 10 y); single-echo labels in 2,504. Quality check: the simulator reproduces Velders' ever-labelled rates (5.2/3.6/4.2% vs 4.6/2.9/3.0% for Capodanno/Dvir/VARC-3) and label persistence; against the true deterioration time the confirmed label had 60% sensitivity, 6% false positives and a 1.5-y median lag.

## 5. Synthetic or Proxy Data (if applicable)

**How.** Each valve has one latent quantity that degenerates, its true effective orifice area. Gradient, peak velocity and DVI follow from flow physics with constants fitted to the ASE 2024 tables. Onset times come from per-valve-class Weibull hazards with published hazard ratios (smoking, diabetes, BMI, CKD, age, anticoagulation). Each echo adds four independent measurement-error sources calibrated to Velders' regression-to-the-mean table. Visits follow the guideline schedule with published missingness; death, endocarditis and detection-dependent reintervention compete. The onset distributions were calibrated so that simulated observed labels match curves reconstructed from Kermen, NOTION and Wakami with each paper's own estimator (all curve fits within 1–3 percentage points). A zero-parameter prediction of NOTION's surgical arm gave 21.6% vs the published 20.7% at 10 y. Full results: `notebooks/simulator/output/calibration_report.md` and `validation_report.md`.

**Limitations.** Published hazard ratios on noisy labels are not biological effects, so three emergent hazard ratios (baseline gradient, small TAVR size, smoking) are weaker than published. Symptoms occur only with true deterioration; death is independent of SVD; the transient-thrombosis gradient effect and the NOTION surgical valve mix are assumptions; there are no site or vendor effects.

**Replacement.** The same code runs on real data because the tables use the registry schema: point `notebooks/analysis/data.py` at an echo measurement export plus STS/TVT fields, re-run `run_all.py`, and keep the simulator for power calculations and for testing surveillance policies before a prospective trial.

## 6. Data Governance and Privacy

Prototype: synthetic data only, generated from published aggregate statistics, so there is no re-identification risk. The provided de-identified extracts were profiled locally and are not used for modelling. All downloaded references carry open licences (`references/README.md`). Production: IRB approval per site; DUAs with STS/ACC for registry data and with CMS/NDI for linkage; HIPAA limited data sets with exact dates, kept inside the covered entity or analysed federatedly; hashed linkage tokens; role-based access and audit logs; no patient-level data in model artefacts; annual re-validation and drift monitoring under a model governance committee.
